# Orva Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This draft must be reviewed by the app owner and hosted at a public HTTPS URL before release.

## Information on your phone

Orva stores family names and optional details, function dates and types, the direction and amount recorded, notes, reminders, language and lock preferences, and local sync information on your phone. PIN verification data is stored in the device's secure storage. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally and contain the family name and function, not amounts.

You can create a JSON backup file and share it using your phone's share sheet. Backup files are not encrypted by Orva. Anyone who can access a backup file can read it.

## Information in the cloud

Orva works without an account. If you sign in and join or start a Family group, family records are sent to the project's Supabase service for shared access and sync. Members of your Family group can see the group's records. The project owner can access cloud records through the Supabase dashboard. Cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted; do not treat it as information that is hidden from the project owner.

Supabase processes account email addresses for sign-in. Orva does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party sale or sharing of family records. Supabase and the email delivery provider process data as necessary to provide cloud sign-in, storage, and sync.

## Your choices and deletion

You can use Orva locally without signing in, turn off reminders, export a backup, restore a backup, leave a Family group, or delete your cloud account from Settings. Account deletion removes your membership; if you are the last member, the group and its cloud records are deleted. The app asks whether to erase the local notebook on this phone as part of account deletion. Shared records remain for other group members when they remain in the group.

Account deletion does not delete backup files you have shared or records that other group members have independently exported. Contact the app owner using the contact details that will be published with the store listing for privacy questions or deletion assistance.

## Data security

The app uses device secure storage for PIN verification and Supabase row-level access policies for cloud groups. These safeguards reduce risk but do not provide end-to-end encryption. Keep your device and backup files protected.

## Changes

The app owner will update this policy if the product's data practices change.
