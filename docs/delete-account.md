# Delete an Orva account (Draft)

This page is a draft for the Play Console account-deletion link. Host it at a public HTTPS URL after owner review.

To delete an Orva cloud account, open **Settings → Family group → Delete cloud account** and confirm the deletion. Orva asks whether to erase the notebook stored on that phone as well.

When other people remain in the Family group, your access is removed and the shared records remain available to those members. If you are the last member, the Family group and its cloud records are deleted. A local notebook is erased only when you choose the phone-data option. Backup files already shared outside Orva are not deleted by this action.

If you cannot open the app, contact the Orva app owner using the support contact published on the store listing and request account deletion. Include the email address used to sign in. Do not send family records or a backup file unless the owner specifically needs it to resolve your request.
